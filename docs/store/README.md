## Listing copy

**Title** RecordPOV

**Apple subtitle** Reaction Video Studio

**Google Play short description** Record reactions, trim your take, add captions, and share from your phone.

**Full description**

RecordPOV is a pocket studio for reactions, commentary, and picture-in-picture videos. Start with a video or photo from your device. On Android you can also start with a supported public video link. Use sources you have permission to use.

Choose a movable camera overlay, split screen, or You on Stage. Remove your camera background on your device. Pause or seek the source while your camera and voice keep recording.

Trim your take, adjust your voice and the original audio separately, and add music. Generate captions from your microphone audio on your device and edit the text before sharing.

Find unfinished takes in My videos. Save and share without an account on iPhone. Sign in to save a finished video on Android. Recordings and drafts stay on your device.

**Listing URLs** [Website](https://recordpov.app/) · [Support](https://recordpov.app/support/) · [Privacy policy](https://recordpov.app/privacy/) · [Account deletion](https://recordpov.app/delete-account/)

## Submission status

- **Apple.** [Version 1.0 build 2](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/apps/6832296716/distribution/reviewsubmissions/details/47464240-0bd0-49e4-a67a-974b93b6109a) was submitted November 12, 2026 and is Waiting for Review. The iPhone release imports from Photos and can save and share without sign-in. The price is free and release is manual.
- **Google Play.** The signed Android 1.0 bundle for `studio.roast.app` is at `apps/android/app/build/outputs/bundle/release/RecordPOV-1.0.aab`. The Play Console requires trader status, physical Android device verification, and contact phone verification before enabling app creation. Add the Play App Signing certificate to the production Google OAuth client and Android App Links configuration when Play supplies it.
- **Support.** The website describes in-app account deletion. Add a monitored public support email and a deletion request option that works outside the app before submitting the Play listing. Google requires that option when users can create accounts. [Google Play account deletion requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111)
