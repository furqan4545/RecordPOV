## Listing copy

**Title** RecordPOV

**Apple subtitle** Reaction Video Studio

**Google Play short description** Record reactions, trim your take, add captions, and share from your phone.

**Full description**

RecordPOV is a pocket studio for reactions, commentary, and picture-in-picture videos. Start with a video or photo from your device or a supported public video link. Use sources you have permission to use.

Choose a movable camera overlay, split screen, or You on Stage. Remove your camera background on your device. Pause or seek the source while your camera and voice keep recording.

Trim your take, adjust your voice and the original audio separately, and add music. Generate captions from your microphone audio on your device and edit the text before sharing.

Find unfinished takes in My videos. Sign in with Apple or Google to save and share the finished video. Recordings and drafts stay on your device.

**Listing URLs** [Website](https://recordpov.app/) · [Support](https://recordpov.app/support/) · [Privacy policy](https://recordpov.app/privacy/) · [Account deletion](https://recordpov.app/delete-account/)

## Submission status

- **Apple.** The [version 1.0 draft](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/apps/6832296716/distribution/ios/version/inflight) has build 1, the screenshots, listing copy, privacy label, age rating, export-compliance answer, and reviewer notes. The price is free and release is manual. Apple requires its Content Rights declaration and reviewer credentials before adding the version for review. The account holder also has an updated Paid Applications Schedule notice in App Store Connect.
- **Google Play.** The Android 1.0 bundle for `studio.roast.app` builds successfully. The signed-in Google account opens developer registration. The bundle still needs upload signing. Add the Play App Signing certificate to the production Google OAuth client and Android App Links configuration when Play supplies it.
- **Support.** The website describes in-app account deletion. Add a monitored public support email and a deletion request option that works outside the app before submitting the Play listing. Google requires that option when users can create accounts. [Google Play account deletion requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111)
