Privacy policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

Videos and audio

RecordPOV uses your camera and microphone when you choose to record. It uses your photo library when you choose a file or grant permission to save a copy. Recordings, drafts, locally imported media, and finished videos are stored on your device. Background removal and speech recognition run on the device. If you share a video, the destination you select handles its own copy.

When you import from a video link, the link is sent to the media service to resolve the source. When server processing is needed, the service temporarily caches a copy of that source and its link. The service expires cached sources automatically. The service uses your IP address to limit abusive requests and maintain some import sessions.

Accounts

RecordPOV uses Apple or Google to sign you in. We store the account identifier and profile information the provider supplies, which can include your name, email address and profile image. We also store session information so you can stay signed in. For Apple accounts, we store an encrypted grant so we can validate or revoke the sign-in. Account information is used to operate sign-in and saving. Signing in does not upload your recordings or create a cloud library.

When you use Google sign-in, Google processes information to authenticate the account, prevent fraud, and measure how sign-in works. This can include user and device identifiers, sign-in activity, an approximate location inferred from your IP address, and a phone number if Google uses it to verify your account.

Website

This website uses no analytics tags or advertising cookies. Our hosting and network providers process requests to deliver the site, including information such as your IP address and browser details.

Launch email

If you ask for a launch notification, we store your email address and the signup time in private storage. We use that list to email you when RecordPOV launches. Signing up for the launch email is separate from creating a RecordPOV account.

Retention and deletion

Account records are kept while your account is active. Signing out revokes the current session when the service is reachable. Deleting your account removes your account and sessions from the account service. Copies on your device, including exports to Photos or Gallery, are managed separately. See Delete your account for instructions.
