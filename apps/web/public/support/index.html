Recording and editing

How do I start a take?

Open Create, choose a video or photo from your device, and open Studio. Choose a camera layout and tap Record. Finish the take to open the editor.

Why can’t RecordPOV use my camera or microphone?

Open your device settings, find RecordPOV, and allow camera and microphone access. Return to Studio to try again.

Where are my drafts and finished videos?

Open My videos. Unfinished takes are saved as drafts. Sign in when prompted to save a finished video and share it from the app.

How do captions work?

Open Captions in the editor to generate text from your microphone audio. Captioning runs on your device. The app may need to download a language or speech model first.
