Delete your account

You can delete your RecordPOV account from the app. Account deletion removes your profile and all RecordPOV sessions and revokes the associated Apple sign-in grant if you used Apple.

Delete from the app

  1. On iPhone or iPad, open Create and tap your profile picture. On Android, open Settings and choose Account.
  2. Choose Delete account and confirm.
  3. Sign in again with the same provider if the app asks you to verify your identity.

Remove local copies

Account deletion keeps your recordings, drafts and exports on your device. To remove them, delete the items from My videos and remove any copies you saved to Photos, Gallery or another destination.
