THE WORKFLOW

From clip to finished take.

  1. 01 / RECORD

    Get in the shot.

    Pick a video or photo. Put your camera in a corner, split the screen, or take center stage with a background cutout.

  2. 02 / EDIT

    Make it your take.

    Trim to the good part. Balance your voice, source audio, and music. Add captions that are generated on your device.

  3. 03 / SHARE

    Send it out.

    Sign in to save your finished video, then share it from your phone. Find your recordings and drafts again in My videos.
